## Active roster and gameday actives

Teams carry a 53-player active roster during the regular season. Only 46 or 48
players may be active for a given game; the rest are declared inactive.

## Injured reserve

A player placed on injured reserve, or IR, is removed from the active roster. A
player placed on IR after the roster is set may return after missing a minimum
number of games, and each team may return only a limited number of players per
season. A player placed on IR before the season begins is generally out for the
year.

## Physically unable to perform

The physically unable to perform list, or PUP, is for players injured during
football activity who have not practised. A player on the preseason PUP list
may be activated at any point in training camp. A player on the regular-season
PUP list must miss a set number of weeks before he is eligible to practise.

## Non-football injury

The non-football injury list, or NFI, applies to players whose injury did not
occur during team football activity. It operates similarly to PUP.

## Practice squad and elevations

The practice squad is a separate group of players who practise with the team
but are not on the active roster. A practice-squad player may be elevated to
play in a game a limited number of times per season without being signed to the
active roster, and reverts afterwards.

## Suspensions and exempt lists

A suspended player does not count against the active roster and may not play or
practise during the suspension. The commissioner exempt list holds players who
are neither active nor suspended, typically during a pending investigation.

## Why roster status is a web-retrieval question

Roster designations change daily and are not statistics. The analytics layer
records what a player did in games that were played; whether he is on IR today
is current information and belongs to web retrieval.
