## Statistical abbreviations

- ATT — attempts
- CMP — completions
- YDS — yards
- TD — touchdown
- INT — interception
- REC — receptions
- TGT — targets
- YPC — yards per carry
- YPR — yards per reception
- YPA — yards per attempt
- RZ — red zone
- FPTS — fantasy points
- PPG — points per game

## Advanced abbreviations

- EPA — expected points added
- CPOE — completion percentage over expected
- aDOT — average depth of target
- YAC — yards after catch
- YBC — yards before catch
- WOPR — weighted opportunity rating
- YPRR — yards per route run

## Fantasy abbreviations

- PPR — points per reception
- TEP — tight end premium
- FLEX — a roster slot fillable by more than one position
- SF — superflex
- DFS — daily fantasy sports
- ADP — average draft position
- WW — waiver wire
- FAAB — free agent acquisition budget

## Roster and availability abbreviations

- IR — injured reserve
- PUP — physically unable to perform
- NFI — non-football injury
- PS — practice squad
- DNP — did not participate
- LP — limited participation
- FP — full participation
- Q — questionable
- D — doubtful
- O — out

## Position abbreviations

QB quarterback, RB running back, FB fullback, WR wide receiver, TE tight end,
OL offensive line, LT left tackle, LG left guard, C centre, RG right guard,
RT right tackle, DL defensive line, DE defensive end, DT defensive tackle,
LB linebacker, EDGE edge rusher, CB cornerback, S safety, FS free safety,
SS strong safety, K kicker, P punter, LS long snapper, DST defence and special
teams.

## Season type abbreviations

- REG — regular season
- POST — postseason
- ALL — both regular season and postseason
