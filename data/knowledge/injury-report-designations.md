## Practice participation

During the practice week each listed player is reported with one of three
participation levels:

- DNP — did not participate
- LP — limited participation
- FP — full participation

Practice participation is reported for Wednesday, Thursday and Friday in a
normal week. A player who does not practise all week is markedly less likely to
play than one who progresses from DNP to FP.

## Game status designations

The final injury report assigns a game status:

- Questionable — uncertain to play. The league describes this as roughly a 50%
  chance, but in practice a questionable player plays more often than not.
- Doubtful — unlikely to play, conventionally understood as around 25%.
- Out — will not play.

The Probable designation was removed after the 2015 season and no longer
appears.

## Inactive lists

Each team declares a list of inactive players shortly before kickoff. A player
without a game-status designation can still be inactive, and a player listed as
questionable is confirmed to be playing only when the inactive list is
published.

## Why this data goes stale quickly

Injury designations change through the week and again on game day. A status
retrieved on Wednesday may be wrong by Sunday.

For this reason the current injury status of a player is a web-retrieval
question, not a knowledge-base question. The historical injury report is
available from the analytics layer; what a player's status is right now is not.

## Reading an absence

A player with no injury entry was not on the injury report. That is a fact
about the player, not missing data, and should be reported as such.
